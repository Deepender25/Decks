ROBUST POLICY LEARNING • SIM2REAL

Injecting Noise
at Input in DRL

Why adding controlled random variance to state observations prevents neural policies from collapsing when confronted with imperfect real-world sensors.

“We deliberately perturb the agent's observation stream so that it learns to extract patterns rather than overfitting to exact, brittle numbers.”
01 / Sim2Real Gap Simulations are noise-free; real robot sensors oscillate by ±2–5%.
02 / Policy Smoothing Forces the actor network πθ to remain invariant to small input jitter.
